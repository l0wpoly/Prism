Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

What we handle. Your messages, any images you send, and any custom instructions you give the AI. Your settings, display name, saved chats and saved AI personalities stay in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers. If you create an account, your account name and password (the password is stored scrambled, never in plain text) are kept by our account provider, Supabase. Accounts don't use an email address, so a forgotten password can't be reset.

How your messages are used. They go to our server and then to Google's Gemini API, which writes the reply. We don't save them on our server. Google processes them under its own terms, and on the free Gemini tier Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Don't send passwords, ID numbers, medical details, private images, or other sensitive or confidential things.

Logs and cookies. Our server may log technical errors, such as a failed request. We don't use tracking cookies.

Your choices. In Settings you can turn off chat saving, clear your chats, or delete everything. Clearing your browser data does the same.

Age. Neo is only for people 18 and older. We ask for your birth year once and only remember whether you're 18 or older, not the year.
