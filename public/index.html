Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

What we handle. The text you submit to a tool or to the assistant chat, plus your settings, display name and edit history. Settings and history are saved in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers. There are no accounts yet.

How your text is used. When you run a tool, your text goes to our server and then to Google's Gemini API, which writes the result. We don't save your text on our server. Google processes it under its own terms, and on the free Gemini tier Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Don't submit passwords, ID numbers, medical details or other sensitive or confidential text.

Logs and cookies. Our server may log technical errors, such as a failed request. We don't use tracking cookies.

Your choices. In Settings you can turn off history, clear it, download your data, or delete everything. Clearing your browser data does the same.

Children. Prism is not meant for children under 13.
